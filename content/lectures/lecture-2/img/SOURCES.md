# Lecture 2 photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons. Local JPEGs retain the source pixels; images
are fitted without cropping in the slides. No real organization's security
configuration is inferred from the photographs.

## airport-checkpoint.jpg

- Subject/credit: Wuhan Tianhe Airport, Terminal 2 · Zheng Zhou · 2024.
- Source: [A Boundary You Have Walked Through](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Security_Checkpoint_(C)_at_ZHHH_Terminal_2.jpg>).
- License: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Teaching point: The checkpoint controls passage between areas. In our grade service, the write endpoint is where permission must be checked.

## response-exercise.jpg

- Subject/credit: Louisiana emergency-response exercise · Robert Kaufmann / FEMA · 2006.
- Source: [Practice Before the Emergency](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FEMA_-_24582_-_Photograph_by_Robert_Kaufmann_taken_on_05-23-2006_in_Louisiana.jpg).
- License: [Public domain (US government)](https://www.fema.gov/photo-video-audio-use-guidelines).
- Teaching point: A disaster-response exercise illustrates the same preparation problem: people, communications, and authority must work together before an incident.

## nersc-tape-library.jpg

- Subject/credit: StorageTek tape library at NERSC · D. Coetzee · 2011.
- Source: [Storage Is Only One Part of Recovery](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Interior_of_StorageTek_tape_library_at_NERSC_(2).jpg>).
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Teaching point: Tape makes the extra copy tangible. A cartridge inside a connected library is not automatically offline or off-site; recovery still needs a tested restore.
